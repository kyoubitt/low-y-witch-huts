# Sources

- **kyoubitt/Low-Witch-Finder**: huts found by the finder, and a hand-collected list ("known list" in `source`).
- **team109/ultra_low_swamp_hut**: its `data/huts/1.21.4/y-55.jsonl` to `y-59.jsonl` (605 entries) were taken as candidates and all checked
  against Minecraft 1.21.4's world generation. 594 reproduced at -55 or deeper (2 of those were already in our own list). 592 are included with `"source":"team109/ultra_low_swamp_hut"`.
  The depth in this repo is the one the check read.
